# Untitled_N80

- **GitHub**: [@nadyasuwarno](https://github.com/nadyasuwarno)
- **Repo**: https://github.com/nadyasuwarno/Linux-LLMs
- **Device**: Nokia N80
- **Model(s)**: Claude (Co-author)
- **Status**: concept 

I want to re-purpose Nokia N80 to become a talking image-recognition camera that identifies objects and people in its field of view. 
N80 is transformed into a networked AI camera that sends a low-resolution image stream to my Mac, where YOLO recognizes objects and MediaPipe analyzes facial expressions. The interpreted images and spoken observations are returned to the Nokia in near real time, allowing the obsolete phone to display and narrate what it believes it sees.
