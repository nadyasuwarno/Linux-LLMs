# Untitles_N80

- **GitHub**: [@nadyasuwarno](https://github.com/nadyasuwarno)
- **Repo**: https://github.com/nadyasuwarno/Linux-LLMs
- **Device**: Nokia N80
- **Model(s)**: Claude (Co-author)
- **Status**: concept 

The Nokia N80 becomes a talking image-recognition camera that identifies objects and people in its field of view. It displays its interpreted image and speaks descriptions such as, “This is a chair,” directly through the phone.
